What is SANA?

SANA (Search Agent Navigation Ablation framework) diagnoses why LLM agents fail at Exploratory QA over data lakes. It converts solved tasks into runtime profiles — a gold source sequence, sanitized subquestions, and execution records — and uses them to build idealized Planning, Search, and Data-Analysis tools. Ablating one component at a time isolates its contribution; the gap that remains when everything is idealized is attributable to the agent's action policy. See the Method page for details.
